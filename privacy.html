Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

This page explains what information zevsocialconnect.com collects and how it's used. Questions? Email zevdod.design@gmail.com.

Information you send us

When you submit the free sample request, design order or onboarding form, the details you type (such as your name, business, email, order and project details) are sent to us by email through Formspree, a form-delivery service. We use them only to reply to you and complete your project. We don't sell or share your information.

Uploads and payments

File uploads go to our Dropbox file request, and payments are processed by PayPal. Those services handle your files and payment details under their own privacy policies. We never see your card number.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) and/or Google Analytics to understand which pages are visited and which buttons are used, for example how many people request free samples. This helps us improve the site. You can block analytics with your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker, and the site will still work.

Local storage

The onboarding form saves your answers in your own browser while you type, so you don't lose your progress. That data stays on your device until you submit or tap "Start over".

Social media access

For social media management clients, we access your Facebook and Instagram through Meta Business Suite partner access. We never ask for or store your passwords, and you can remove our access anytime.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
