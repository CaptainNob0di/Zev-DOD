40 Social Media Post Ideas for Local Small Businesses

By Isaiah Lewis, Zev DOD · November 23, 2026 · 3 min read

Most small businesses don't stop posting because they're lazy. They stop because they run out of ideas on a busy Tuesday. The fix is a list you can pull from. Here are 40 ideas, grouped by what each post is meant to do.

A good mix for most local businesses is roughly: a few posts that show your work, a few that teach something, a few that show the people behind it, a few that start a conversation, and only some that sell directly.

Show your work (proof)

  1. A before-and-after photo of a real job.
  2. Your best-selling item, shot up close in natural light.
  3. A finished project with a one-line story of what the customer needed.
  4. A time-lapse of something being made, cut, cleaned or built.
  5. A "today's work" carousel of three to five photos.
  6. A side-by-side of two styles or options you offer.
  7. A real customer review, shared with their permission, as a graphic.
  8. Your workspace, ready for the day.

Teach something (education)

  1. One tip that makes your product or service last longer.
  2. A common mistake customers make, and how to avoid it.
  3. "What to ask before you hire a [your trade]."
  4. A quick explanation of a term your customers don't know.
  5. A seasonal reminder (when to book lawn aeration, when to order holiday pies).
  6. A myth in your industry, and the truth.
  7. How to choose between two of your services.
  8. A three-step how-to carousel.

Behind the scenes (people)

  1. Introduce a team member: name, role, one fun fact.
  2. Your morning setup or prep routine.
  3. A supplier or local partner you love, tagged (with their OK).
  4. The story of why you started the business.
  5. A tool or piece of equipment you can't live without.
  6. Something going wrong and how you fixed it.
  7. A milestone: anniversary, new location, new hire.
  8. A "day in the life" Reel from your phone.

Start a conversation (community)

  1. A this-or-that poll (spicy or mild, fade or taper).
  2. "Wrong answers only" about a photo.
  3. Ask followers what new product or service they want.
  4. Shout out a local event, school team or neighborhood.
  5. Share a throwback of your first day or first product.
  6. Ask a fill-in-the-blank question.
  7. Celebrate a local holiday or Omaha moment.
  8. Thank customers after a busy weekend.

Sell directly (offers)

  1. A limited-time offer with a clear end date.
  2. Your hours, booking link and location in one graphic.
  3. "Only X spots left this week."
  4. A bundle or gift idea for an upcoming holiday.
  5. New arrivals or a new menu item.
  6. A reminder to book ahead for a busy season.
  7. A referral offer for current customers.
  8. Your event: date, time, address, what to expect.

How to turn this list into a month

Pick three posting days you can keep up, for example Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Then rotate:

Week Mon Wed Fri
1 Offer Teach Proof
2 Behind the scenes Proof Community
3 Teach Offer Proof
4 Community Behind the scenes Offer

That is twelve posts, and only three of them ask for a sale. That balance keeps people following instead of tuning out.

Three rules that make any idea work

  • Use real photos whenever you can. Customers want to see your actual shop, food and work.
  • Every post needs one clear next step: book, call, DM, visit, or comment.
  • Only post offers and prices you are sure about. Double-check dates before anything goes live.
