Every design order includes a high-res PNG for social and a print-ready PDF, plus 2 revision rounds (one list of changes counts as one round). Orders under $100 are paid in full up front. For $100 and up, you pay a 50% deposit and the balance before final files. Full refund if your first draft isn't delivered on time. You own the final design after full payment.

How a design order works

  1. Order: pick your design and fill out the form. It takes about 2 minutes.
  2. Pay: pay with PayPal. Your spot is held once payment arrives.
  3. Review: get a fast first draft and up to 2 rounds of changes.
  4. Launch: get your final print and social files.

No calls needed. Everything happens by email, DM or text. Logos are built from original shapes and licensed fonts, so you can register them.

Frequently asked questions

How fast will I get my design?

Flyers, event ads and social sets: first draft within 24 hours of payment. Menus: 24 to 48 hours. Branding concepts: within 48 hours. Rush 6-hour delivery (+$15) is available for single designs.

What if I don't love the draft?

Every order includes 2 revision rounds. Extra rounds are $5 each.

Can you design in Spanish?

Yes. Bilingual English/Spanish flyers and menus at no extra charge.

Do you print?

We deliver print-ready files you can take to any print shop or online printer, and we can recommend sizes and paper.

How do I send my logo and photos?

After you order, use the Upload Files button. Files up to 2 GB each, no account needed.
