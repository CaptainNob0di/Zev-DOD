Questions

Do I need to take photos?

Yes, real photos work best. Share a folder of 15 or more and add new ones whenever you can. For slow weeks we use branded text graphics.

How do you get into my accounts?

You add Zev DOD as a partner in Meta Business Suite (we send 5 simple steps). You never share a password, and you can remove access any time.

How do I pay?

A secure online card subscription, billed monthly on the same date. You can update your card or cancel from the link in your receipt.

What if I don't like a post?

Nothing goes out until you approve the calendar. Each plan includes revision rounds.

How fast can you start?

Your first month of posts is ready for approval within 7 days of your completed questionnaire.
