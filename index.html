Design FAQ

How fast will I get my design?

Flyers, event ads and social sets: first draft within 24 hours of payment. Menus: 24 to 48 hours. Branding concepts: within 48 hours. Rush 6-hour delivery (+$15) is available for single designs.

What if I don't love the draft?

Every order includes 2 revision rounds. Extra rounds are $5 each.

What files do I get?

A social-sized PNG and a print-ready PDF. Want to edit it yourself later? Add an editable Canva link for $10.

Can you do Spanish?

Yes. Bilingual English/Spanish flyers and menus at no extra charge.

Do you print?

We send print-ready files you can take to any print shop or online printer. Ask and we'll recommend sizes and paper.

Refunds?

If your first draft isn't delivered on time, you get a full refund. After a draft is delivered, refunds aren't offered, but your revisions are included.

Who owns the design?

You do, once it's paid in full. Logos use original shapes and licensed fonts so you can register them.
