Frequently asked questions

How much does social media management cost?

Our plans are $150, $300 or $500 a month depending on posts, platforms and video. See our guide to social media management costs for what drives pricing.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Plans are month-to-month with 14 days' notice to cancel.

Do I have to take the photos?

Real photos work best, so share a folder of 15 or more and add new ones when you can. For slow weeks we use branded text graphics.

Do you need my password?

Never. You add Zev DOD as a partner in Meta Business Suite and can remove access anytime.

Do you run paid ads?

Paid ad management isn't included in our plans. We focus on consistent organic posting and branded content.

How fast can you start?

Your first month of posts is ready for approval within 7 days of your completed onboarding questionnaire.
