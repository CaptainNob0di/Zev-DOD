Small Business Saturday Marketing Ideas for Omaha Shops

By Isaiah Lewis, Zev DOD · November 23, 2026 · 3 min read

Small Business Saturday falls on the Saturday after Thanksgiving. In 2026 that's Saturday, November 28. It's one of the few days a year when shoppers go looking for local businesses on purpose. You don't need a big budget to take part. You need a reason to visit, clear details, and a few days of steady promotion.

Pick one simple offer

People respond to offers they can understand at a glance. Pick one:

  • A percentage or dollar discount for the day only
  • A free gift with purchase while supplies last
  • A bundle (two items, one price)
  • A bonus for future visits, like a coupon for January, which brings people back
  • A free add-on for service businesses, such as a free beard trim with any cut

Write down the exact terms before you promote anything: what's included, start and end times, and any limits.

Give people a reason to come in, not just buy

  • Free coffee, cider or treats in the morning
  • A raffle for anyone who visits
  • A local maker, food truck or DJ for an hour
  • A behind-the-scenes demo of how you make what you sell

Team up with neighbors

Omaha's small-business districts, such as Benson, Dundee, Blackstone, the Old Market and South 24th Street, are walkable. Coordinate with two or three nearby shops:

  • Share each other's posts
  • Create a simple "visit all three" stamp card
  • Hang each other's flyers

A one-week promotion plan

Day Post
Sunday Announce the offer with a clear graphic and all details
Monday Behind the scenes: getting ready
Tuesday Spotlight a product or service that makes a good gift
Wednesday Reminder Story with a countdown sticker
Thursday (Thanksgiving) A short thank-you post, no hard sell
Friday "Tomorrow" reminder with hours and address
Saturday Morning "we're open" post, then Stories throughout the day

Pin the announcement post to the top of your Instagram and Facebook profiles for the week.

Don't forget Google

Update your Google Business Profile with special hours and an "Offer" post. Many shoppers search "near me" on the day itself. (New to it? Read how to set up Google Business Profile for an Omaha business.)

Print still works

Put a flyer in your window, by the register, and on community boards a week ahead. Include the date, your hours and the offer. Hand a small card with a January coupon to every customer on the day.

After the day

  • Thank customers in a post on Sunday.
  • Note what sold and which post brought people in. Ask at checkout: "How did you hear about us?"
  • Reuse what worked for your holiday and New Year promotions.

The Omaha holiday calendar keeps going

Small Business Saturday kicks off a busy stretch: holiday markets, office parties and New Year promotions. Plan your December posts now so you aren't scrambling.
