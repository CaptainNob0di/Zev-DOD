HOW TO PLAY

Goal: your team (Perci's crew) vs 5 BROKE-pilot bots. Most tags when the 2:00 clock hits zero wins.

Phone: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires (aim assist helps). Tap an item slot to use a power-up. ◀ ▶ COVER dashes you behind the nearest box on that side. Tap the yellow button for your power. Turn your phone sideways for the widest view.

Desktop: move with WASD or arrows, aim with the mouse, hold click to fire, SPACE for your power, 1-4 to use items, Q / E for cover left / right. P pauses.

Maps: every match builds a brand-new arena (Corn Maze, Building 8 Halls, Might Be Castle Lot, The Barn, Dream Grid, School Gym), so no two games play the same.

Shields: everyone has 3 (Perci has 4). Lose them all and you're tagged out for 3 seconds.

Power-ups (run over them to grab): they go into your 4 item slots so you can use them when you need them. A+ = +1 shield • lightning = rapid fire • arrows = speed boost • star = power recharged. +10 points each. Slots full? Use one to grab more. (Online matches use power-ups right away.)

Score: +100 per tag, +500 for a win, +10 per power-up, plus an accuracy bonus, minus 25 each time you're tagged. Difficulty multiplies it all.

Story Mode (18+): 6 chapters that follow Episodes 1-6, with short animated scenes between levels. Each chapter has its own goal (tag a number of bots, survive the clock, out-tag them, or beat the boss). Clear a chapter to unlock the next; earn up to 3 stars. Watch for Dream Kush (slows everything down) and Might Be Castle munchies (full shields).
