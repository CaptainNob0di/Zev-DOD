HOW TO PLAY

Goal: your team (Perci's crew) vs 5 BROKE-pilot bots. Most tags when the 2:00 clock hits zero wins.

Desktop: move with WASD or arrows, aim with the mouse, hold click to fire, SPACE for your power. P pauses.

Phone: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires (with aim assist). AUTO fires at the closest bot in view when you're only moving. Tap the yellow button for your power. Turn your phone sideways for the widest view.

Maps: every match builds a brand-new arena (Corn Maze, Building 8 Halls, Might Be Castle Lot, The Barn, Dream Grid, School Gym), so no two games play the same.

Shields: everyone has 3 (Perci has 4). Lose them all and you're tagged out for 3 seconds.

Power-ups (just run over them): A+ = +1 shield • lightning = rapid fire • arrows = speed boost • star = power recharged. +10 points each.

Score: +100 per tag, +500 for a win, +10 per power-up, plus an accuracy bonus, minus 25 each time you're tagged. Difficulty multiplies it all.

Online: tap PLAY ONLINE, create a room and share the 4-letter code. The host picks Team up vs bots or Team vs team. Players marked with a star are real people. Tap the X twice to leave.

Leaderboard: post your score under your name and show it off. Top 50 all-time and today's best.
