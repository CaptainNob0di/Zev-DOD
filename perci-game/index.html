HOW TO PLAY

Goal: your team (Perci's crew) vs 5 BROKE-pilot bots. Most tags when the 2:00 clock hits zero wins.

Phone: left thumb moves, yellow AIM stick looks around, red FIRE stick above it shoots (drag toward a bot, or hold the middle for the closest one). Power-ups work the moment you grab them. Tap the yellow button for your power. Turn your phone sideways for the widest view.

Desktop: move with WASD or arrows, aim with the mouse, hold click to fire, SPACE for your power, 1-4 to use items, P pauses.

Maps: every match builds a brand-new arena (Corn Maze, Building 8 Halls, Might Be Castle Lot, The Barn, Dream Grid, School Gym), so no two games play the same.

Shields: everyone has 3 (Perci has 4). Lose them all and you're tagged out for 3 seconds.

Power-ups (run over them to grab): they take effect the moment you grab them. A+ = +1 shield • lightning = rapid fire • arrows = speed boost • star = power recharged. +10 points each.

Score: +100 per tag, +500 for a win, +10 per power-up, plus an accuracy bonus, minus 25 each time you're tagged. Difficulty multiplies it all.

Story Mode (mature content): 6 chapters that follow Episodes 1-6, with short animated scenes between levels. Each chapter has its own goal (tag a number of bots, survive the clock, out-tag them, or beat the boss). Clear a chapter to unlock the next; earn up to 3 stars. Watch for Dream Kush (slows everything down) and Might Be Castle munchies (full shields).

Push the line: every tag moves the center line toward the other team. Push it all the way to their side and you win on the spot. The bar at the bottom shows where it is.

Dream / real: the arena flips between the dream and the real world every 45 seconds, after any 3-tag streak, or when someone grabs a spinning top. In the real world there are no powers or power-ups, everyone is slower (Perci's on crutches), and lasers bounce off walls once.

Arena events: every map has one: lights out, a twister, rogue robot vacuums, BROKE-pilot signal strikes, or the dream rig pulse.

Report card: BROKE-pilot grades you live (A+ stamps for tags, F when you get tagged) and hands you a report card at the end. Save it and share it.

Levels and weapons: every match and chapter gives your fighter XP and ZEV Coins. Each level makes that fighter a bit faster with quicker powers, and every 10 levels adds a shield point. Leveling any fighter unlocks weapons: Buzz SMG (LV 3), Scatter Gun (LV 6), Bounce Launcher (LV 10), Beam Rifle (LV 15). Pick your weapon before a match.
